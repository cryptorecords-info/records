Description
Quantum analogues retaining the baseline pair-sieve list-space exponent.
Comparison rules
Compare sequential time with total classical-list storage at most the baseline exponent, allowing displayed rounding 0.2075/0.208. Batch Nguyen–Vidick variants and GaussSieve variants must be identified: sequential hash-table processing is not automatically valid for GaussSieve. Require coherent access where used and record ordinary-qubit requirements separately.
