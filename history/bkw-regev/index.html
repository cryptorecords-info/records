Description
Historical classical BKW coefficients at the Regev error scale, with abundant fresh input equations.
Comparison rules
Fix prime q ≍ n² and Gaussian error standard deviation σ = q/(√(2πn) (log₂ n)²). Exponentially many independent classical samples with the same secret may be read; sample acquisition is charged. The coordinate distribution and independence assumptions of each analysis remain part of the claim. These bounds are not for a fixed finite sample budget, small binary errors, or a structured ring instance. The score is the leading coefficient of n in log₂ time. Memory bounds are recorded separately when identified. Objective: Decision-LWE with constant target distinguishing advantage. Search-LWE algorithms are admissible as decision upper bounds; a search-only theorem is not silently identified with the source's optimized decision corollary.
