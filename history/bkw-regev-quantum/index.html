Description
Quantum-search variants of coded BKW with abundant classical input samples.
Comparison rules
Use the same Regev modulus, error and fresh classical sample convention as the classical category. Grover-based geometric searches assume coherent indexed reads of their working tables. Record the coherent-access assumption explicitly. A reusable-walk method that requires coherent writes or a stronger distribution model belongs in a separately tagged comparison category; no such unpublished claim is seeded here. Objective: Decision-LWE with constant target distinguishing advantage. Search-LWE algorithms are admissible as decision upper bounds; a search-only theorem is not silently identified with the source's optimized decision corollary.
