Assumptions and source notes

Corollary 3.8 gives unconditional time and space with coefficient 0.6078 + ε_μ, where ε_μ tends to zero as the constant approximation factor μ grows. The stored 0.6078 is the limiting family coefficient; it is not an exact-SVP exponent or a guarantee at every fixed μ. Nearest-neighbor filtering and a perturbation proof give a new provable sieving method. Hhan’s earlier exact-SVP exponent 0.5 already implies a faster constant-approximation bound.
