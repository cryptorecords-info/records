Assumptions and source notes

FOCS 2015. Randomized exact CVP on arbitrary n-dimensional Euclidean lattices and arbitrary targets; total time and space 2^{n+o(n)}.

Selected earlier references

Selected sources cited by this paper.

  1. Solving the Shortest Vector Problem in 2^n Time via Discrete Gaussian Sampling

    Divesh Aggarwal · Daniel Dadush · Oded Regev · Noah Stephens-Davidowitz · 2015

    Identifies the earlier discrete-Gaussian approximation result before the exact 2^n CVP algorithm. Introduction, 1.97-approximate CVP discussion

  2. Sampling short lattice vectors and the closest lattice vector problem

    Miklós Ajtai · Ravi Kumar · D. Sivakumar · 2002

    Traces the first randomized near-exact CVP approximation algorithm. Introduction and references [AKS02]

  3. Solving the Shortest Vector Problem in 2^n Time via Discrete Gaussian Sampling

    Divesh Aggarwal · Daniel Dadush · Oded Regev · Noah Stephens-Davidowitz · 2015

    Extends centered Gaussian-combining techniques to shifted distributions with a distance-relative cutoff. Abstract; §4
