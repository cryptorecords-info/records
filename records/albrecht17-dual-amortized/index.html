LWE / Dual lattice · parameterized models

On dual lattice attacks against small-secret LWE and parameter choices in HElib and SEAL

Martin R. Albrecht

Eurocrypt 2017

PublishedSource checked
Time bound
TBKZ(m,β) + M·[Trerandomize(m) + TBKZ(m,β′)], with β′≤β
Space bound
Reduced basis plus M short dual vectors, if stored.
Source / version date
18 May 2017
Comparison variant
Dual lattice · parameterized models
Cost scale
Parameter-dependent time and space expressions
Parameters
Fix n,q, Gaussian width αq, lattice dimension m, block sizes β,β′ and required relation count M.
Error distribution
Discrete Gaussian width αq.
Secret distribution
General LWE secret; this amortization does not require a short secret.
Sample budget
Works on the available LWE matrix; reuses it, unlike fully independent fresh-sample amplification.
Objective
Decision-LWE with amplified distinguishing advantage.
Cost model
One BKZ-β preprocessing followed by sparse unimodular rerandomization and cheaper BKZ-β′; source instantiation β′=2.
Assumptions
Cheap rerandomization yields short, adequately random relations; dependence can be ignored in the statistical test.
Success guarantee
Target advantage determines M; source estimates use dual-vector norm inflation by a factor of two.

Assumptions and source notes

Reuses an expensive reduced basis to generate many shorter dual relations. The claimed reduction relies on relations being sufficiently short and sufficiently random; fixed-sample correlations remain a heuristic.

Source location

Section Amortising costs, Algorithm 1 and its Heuristic paragraph

Selected earlier references

Selected sources cited by this paper.

  1. Better Key Sizes (and Attacks) for LWE-Based Encryption

    Richard Lindner · Chris Peikert · 2011

    Explains why extrapolating the empirical BKZ seconds model gives misleading asymptotic LWE estimates. Preliminaries, LP model subsection; reference RSA:LinPei11 in author source

  2. On the concrete hardness of Learning with Errors

    Martin R. Albrecht · Rachel Player · Sam Scott · 2015

    Refines earlier dual attack costing by amortizing generation of short dual relations. Preliminaries and Amortising costs; reference JMC:AlbPlaSco15

  3. Lattice Decoding Attacks on Binary LWE

    Shi Bai · Steven D. Galbraith · 2014

    Applies the earlier secret/error lattice scaling idea to a dual attack. Introduction and Modulus switching section; reference ACISP:BaiGal14
