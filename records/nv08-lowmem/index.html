Assumptions and source notes

Heuristic AKS variant: limiting time coefficient log₂(4/3) ≈ 0.4150 and space coefficient ½log₂(4/3) ≈ 0.2075. The source states (4/3+ε)^n time for every fixed ε>0. Chronology uses the 11 September 2008 online-publication date reported by the institutional repository, https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/etxts-sdp80; it does not assert the earliest circulated draft date.
