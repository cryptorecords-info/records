Assumptions and source notes

The initial arXiv version explicitly states the classical 2.05 time / 0.5 space point. The coefficient is retained at the precision actually checked in v1; a more precise value from the 2018 revision is not silently backdated. This illustrates a smaller-space trade-off after the 2^n-time result, not a new fastest time.
