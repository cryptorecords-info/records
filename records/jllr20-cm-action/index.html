CM action / Quantum · cyclic class group · polynomial qubits

A subexponential-time, polynomial quantum space algorithm for inverting the CM group action

David Jao · Jason LeGrow · Christopher Leonardi · Luis Ruiz-Lopez

Journal of Mathematical Cryptology 2020

PublishedSource checked

Assumptions and source notes

The checked analysis assumes the class group is cyclic. It uses classical subexponential precomputation and storage, but polynomial quantum space for the online action-inversion computation. Extension to non-cyclic groups is conjectured in Section 3.1.
