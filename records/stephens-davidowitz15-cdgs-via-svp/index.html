Assumptions and source notes

Historical claim of an expected-polynomial-time, dimension-preserving reduction from arbitrary-width centered DGS to exact SVP on sublattices. Instantiating the claimed reduction with ADRS15 gives coefficient 1, without improving the already known DGS exponent 1. The first arXiv version contains the stated reduction and accuracy guarantee, but GHH26 Appendix D.1 identifies a counterexample to the additive squared-radius grid comparison in this proof; its multiplicative-grid replacement repairs the reduction in 2026. This entry is therefore disputed and excluded from admitted historical bounds and result markers. The exponent 1 itself remains independently supported by the ADRS15 sampler; the issue concerns this particular reduction proof. The multiplicative accuracy is inverse polynomial, not negligible TV error.
