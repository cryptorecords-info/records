LWE / Gaussian error · algebraic bounds

New Algorithms for Learning in Presence of Errors

Sanjeev Arora · Rong Ge

ICALP 2011

PublishedSource checked
Time bound
2^{Õ(n2ε)}, for αq=n^ε and fixed ε<1/2
Space bound
Exponential-size linearization; no numerical space coefficient extracted.
Source / version date
13 Dec 2010
Comparison variant
Gaussian error · algebraic bounds
Cost scale
Parameter-dependent time and space expressions
Parameters
Prime q=q(n) with q ≫ (αq log n)²; αq=n^ε, fixed 0≤ε<1/2.
Error distribution
Source Gaussian Ψ_α over GF(q), width parameter αq.
Secret distribution
Arbitrary fixed u∈GF(q)^n.
Sample budget
Oracle access; 2^{Õ(n^{2ε})} samples allowed, not a linear-sample claim.
Objective
Recover the full secret.
Cost model
Classical randomized polynomial-system linearization; polynomial input-size factors implicit.
Assumptions
Theorem for the specified Gaussian distribution and modulus regime.
Success guarantee
High probability as in Theorems 3.1 and 3.3.

Assumptions and source notes

Subexponential only in the stated small-error regime. Date is the checked ECCC revision, preceding ICALP 2011.

Source location

§3, Theorem 3.1, p. 8; construction and Theorem 3.3
Version: ECCC TR10-066 revision 1, 13 December 2010
