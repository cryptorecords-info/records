Selected earlier references

Selected sources cited by this paper.

  1. Sieve Algorithms for the Shortest Vector Problem are Practical

    Phong Q. Nguyen · Thomas Vidick · 2008

    Quantum search applied to the earlier heuristic sieve. Section 2.1, Lemma 1 and Theorem 1; reference [41]

  2. Solving the Shortest Lattice Vector Problem in Time 2^(2.465n)

    Xavier Pujol · Damien Stehlé · 2009

    Quantum speedup of the birthday saturation algorithm. Section 3.1, Lemma 2 and Theorem 2; reference [44]

  3. Improved Nguyen-Vidick Heuristic Sieve Algorithm for Shortest Vector Problem

    Xiaoyun Wang · Mingjie Liu · Chengliang Tian · Jingguo Bi · 2011

    Studies a two-level sieve and finds no quantum exponent gain over the baseline. Section 2.2; reference [57]
