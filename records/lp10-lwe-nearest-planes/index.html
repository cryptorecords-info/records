LWE / Lattice decoding · parameterized models

Better Key Sizes (and Attacks) for LWE-Based Encryption

Richard Lindner · Chris Peikert

CT-RSA 2011

PublishedSource checked
Time bound
Treduce(B) + poly(m) ∏i=1m di
Space bound
Polynomial working space for depth-first search; the ∏di output set need not all be retained.
Source / version date
30 Nov 2010
Comparison variant
Lattice decoding · parameterized models
Cost scale
Parameter-dependent time and space expressions
Parameters
Fix n,q,m,s, reduced basis B and positive integer branching factors d_1,…,d_m.
Error distribution
Continuous Gaussian D_s with density proportional to exp(−π||e||²/s²); wide discrete Gaussian approximated by it.
Secret distribution
General LWE secret; lattice target is As+e mod q.
Sample budget
m given LWE samples; no unlimited-sample substitution.
Objective
Recover the error and secret by lattice decoding.
Cost model
NearestPlanes visits ∏d_i leaves, each polynomial work; include basis reduction in total time.
Assumptions
Success probability formula uses independent Gaussian projections; basis-profile estimates can additionally use GSA.
Success guarantee
P = ∏_i erf(d_i ||b_i*||√π/(2s)); choose d to meet an explicit target P.

Assumptions and source notes

The post-reduction search cost is a tradeoff in the branching vector d. The Gaussian success formula is exact for continuous noise; transferring it to discrete LWE is an approximation. The original empirical BKZ seconds fit is not treated as an asymptotic bound.

Source location

§4, Lemma 4.1, p. 11, and Eq. (4.1), p. 12
Version: Author manuscript dated 30 November 2010
