Assumptions and source notes

A low-memory improvement, rather than a chronological time record after SphereSieve. The source states heuristic time 0.3112 while retaining space 0.2075. The method adapts nearest-neighbor search for binary codes. First ePrint received 31 May 2015; source publication status is preprint.
