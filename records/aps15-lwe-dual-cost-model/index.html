LWE / Dual lattice · parameterized models

On the concrete hardness of Learning with Errors

Martin R. Albrecht · Rachel Player · Sam Scott

Journal of Mathematical Cryptology 2015

PublishedSource checked
Time bound
O(ε⁻² · TBKZ(m,δ₀)); log₂δ₀=[log₂(f(ε)/α)]²/(4nlog₂q), f(ε)=√(ln(1/ε)/π)
Space bound
Depends on the chosen SVP oracle inside BKZ.
Source / version date
2 Oct 2015
Comparison variant
Dual lattice · parameterized models
Cost scale
Parameter-dependent time and space expressions
Parameters
Fix n,q(n), Gaussian width αq, target one-vector bias ε and BKZ cost model; enough samples for optimized m.
Error distribution
Discrete Gaussian width αq, standard deviation αq/√(2π).
Secret distribution
General LWE secret in Z_q^n.
Sample budget
Unlimited samples in this historical survey; m≈√(nlog₂q/log₂δ₀) per fresh dual-vector instance, repeated for amplification.
Objective
Distinguish LWE from uniform with constant final advantage.
Cost model
||v||≈δ₀^m q^{n/m}; bias≈exp(−π(α||v||)²); β/log₂β≈1/(2log₂δ₀). Select the SVP-oracle cost explicitly.
Assumptions
Lattice-reduction/Gaussian heuristic model and independent repetitions.
Success guarantee
One-vector bias ε; O(ε⁻²) independent observations for constant final advantage.

Assumptions and source notes

Historical dual model combining target vector length with a stated BKZ oracle. Its rule-of-thumb relation β/log₂β≈1/(2log₂δ₀) avoids falsely extrapolating an empirical seconds fit into a subexponential LWE algorithm.

Source location

§3.2, Corollary 3.2 / Remark 3.3; §4.1, Lemma 4.1; §5.3, Lemma 5.12 and following amplification discussion
Version: Journal of Mathematical Cryptology 9(3), published 2 October 2015

Selected earlier references

Selected sources cited by this paper.

  1. New Algorithms for Learning in Presence of Errors

    Sanjeev Arora · Rong Ge · 2011

    Analyzes the small-error algebraic linearization algorithm as one LWE solving strategy. §5.6, Arora–Ge and Gröbner bases; reference [12]

  2. Better Key Sizes (and Attacks) for LWE-Based Encryption

    Richard Lindner · Chris Peikert · 2011

    Optimizes and compares reduction plus nearest-planes decoding costs. §5.4 and reference [52]

  3. On the Efficacy of Solving LWE by Reduction to Unique-SVP

    Martin R. Albrecht · Robert Fitzpatrick · Florian Göpfert · 2014

    Imports the embedding gap estimate into its primal LWE cost model. §5.5, Lemma 5.17 attributed to [10, Lemma 2]

  4. Lattice Decoding Attacks on Binary LWE

    Shi Bai · Steven D. Galbraith · 2014

    Analyzes the earlier small-secret rescaled embedding in a common cost framework. §6.3, Lemma 6.4 and reference [14]
