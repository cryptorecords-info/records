Assumptions and source notes

STOC 2015. Randomized exact Euclidean SVP via DGS; time and space 2^{n+o(n)}. The chronology uses the first arXiv submission date.

Selected earlier references

Selected sources cited by this paper.

  1. A Deterministic Single Exponential Time Algorithm for Most Lattice Problems Based on Voronoi Cell Computations

    Daniele Micciancio · Panagiotis Voulgaris · 2013

    Uses the previous exact-CVP/Voronoi bound as the comparison point for 1.97-approximate CVP. Abstract; introduction

  2. On the Closest Vector Problem with a Distance Guarantee

    Daniel Dadush · Oded Regev · Noah Stephens-Davidowitz · 2014

    Combines the distance-guaranteed decoder with above-smoothing DGS to charge construction of the Gaussian advice and obtain 0.422-BDD in half-exponent total time. §7.2, Theorem 7.3 explicitly attributed to DRS14 Theorem 3.1
