Assumptions and source notes

Reciprocal Korkin–Zolotarev bases yield the classical O(n^{3/2}) search-CVPP approximation. The original transference and closest-point bounds are in Theorem 2.7 and §6; the preprocessing interpretation is explicitly described by Regev §I and DRS §1.

Source location

Theorem 2.7 and §6; interpretation cross-checked in Regev 2004 §I and DRS 2014 §1
Version: Combinatorica 10(4), 1990
