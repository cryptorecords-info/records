Assumptions and source notes

The HP technical report (October 1997), subsequently published in Journal of Cryptology (1999), gives the p-adic attack for #E(F_p)=p. Its stated linear algorithm is not translated into a bit-complexity exponent here. This exceptional-family attack does not solve general ECDLP.
