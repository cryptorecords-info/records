Assumptions and source notes

Version 2 states the 5/2 leading coefficient for the family of ℓp norms. Its arithmetic bound is retained with the version’s weaker space and intermediate-bit-size documentation.

Selected earlier references

Selected sources cited by this paper.

  1. Closest Vectors, Successive Minima, and Dual HKZ-Bases of Lattices

    Johannes Blömer · 2000

    States the earlier exact Euclidean n! algorithm and its polynomial-space bound. §1, algorithms for CVP, reference [Blö00]

  2. Improved Analysis of Kannan's Shortest Lattice Vector Algorithm

    Guillaume Hanrot · Damien Stehlé · 2007

    Compares its general-norm algorithm with Euclidean polynomial-space enumeration. §1, algorithms for CVP, reference [HS07]

  3. A Deterministic Single Exponential Time Algorithm for Most Lattice Problems Based on Voronoi Cell Computations

    Daniele Micciancio · Panagiotis Voulgaris · 2013

    Distinguishes its polynomial-space general-norm algorithm from Euclidean Voronoi computation. §1, algorithms for CVP, reference [MV10]
