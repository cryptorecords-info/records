Assumptions and source notes

Exact ℓ∞-SVP using the birthday variant, with γ = 0.67 and ξ = 0.868. The coefficient was checked in v2; the row conservatively uses that version’s 15 May 2018 arXiv date rather than assuming it was already in v1 on 8 January. It is not a Euclidean-SVP coefficient.
