LWE / Dual lattice · parameterized models

Lattice-based Cryptography

Daniele Micciancio · Oded Regev

Post-Quantum Cryptography 2009

PublishedSource checked
Time bound
Treduce(m, δ); m ≈ sqrt(n log₂(q) / log₂(δ))
Space bound
Depends on the selected lattice-reduction algorithm.
Source / version date
7 Nov 2008
Comparison variant
Dual lattice · parameterized models
Cost scale
Parameter-dependent time and space expressions
Parameters
Prime q=q(n); secret dimension n; root-Hermite factor δ; choose m only when enough samples are available.
Error distribution
Rounded Gaussian width αq; continuous standard deviation αq/√(2π).
Secret distribution
General LWE secret in Z_q^n.
Sample budget
At least m ≈ √(n log₂ q / log₂ δ); cap m by the actual sample budget.
Objective
Distinguish LWE from uniform using a short dual vector.
Cost model
Unscaled dual length ≈ q⁻¹ min(q, 2^{2√(n log₂(q) log₂(δ))}); source Eqs. (10)–(11).
Assumptions
Empirical lattice-reduction quality model for random q-ary lattices.
Success guarantee
Non-negligible distinguishing signal requires suitably small αq·||w||; no common fixed advantage is imposed here.

Assumptions and source notes

Early explicit dual-LWE geometry model. Shortness and dimension predictions do not supply a universal LWE running-time exponent.

Source location

§5, Security discussion, pp. 21–22, Eqs. (10)–(11)
Version: Author manuscript dated 7 November 2008; edited chapter February 2009
