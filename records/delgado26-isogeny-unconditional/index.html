Assumptions and source notes

The source abstract claims a Las Vegas OneEnd algorithm analyzed without a smoothness heuristic, with the displayed expected time and memory. Known unconditional reductions transfer OneEnd to the supersingular endomorphism-ring and isogeny problems. This is a preprint claim checked against the primary arXiv abstract; the full proof was not independently audited. The p exponent remains exponential in log p and is not a concrete parameter-set break.
