Assumptions and source notes

The batch is smaller than the 2^{n/2} outputs in ADRS15; its total cost is kept in a separate category.

Selected earlier references

Selected sources cited by this paper.

  1. One Discrete Gaussian Sample in 2^{n/2+o(n)} Time

    Jiseung Kim · 2026

    Identifies the preceding half-exponent algorithm for one centered sample at arbitrary width. §1.1 literature discussion

  2. Discrete Gaussian Sampling Reduces to CVP and SVP

    Noah Stephens-Davidowitz · 2016

    Uses the centered-to-SVP reduction framework, reports a radius-grid problem in its proof, and supplies a corrected argument. Corollary 1.4; §12.4; Appendix D

  3. On the Closest Vector Problem with a Distance Guarantee

    Daniel Dadush · Oded Regev · Noah Stephens-Davidowitz · 2014

    Uses dual Gaussian batches as reusable advice for the periodic-Gaussian decoder while accounting for preprocessing and query costs. §12.2; Theorem 12.1 proof; Appendix F.1
