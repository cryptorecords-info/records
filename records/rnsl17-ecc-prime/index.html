Quantum Resource Estimates for Computing Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithms

Martin Roetteler · Michael Naehrig · Krysta M. Svore · Kristin Lauter

Asiacrypt 2017

PublishedSource checked
Time bound
At most 448 n3 log₂(n) + 4090 n3 logical Toffoli gates
Space bound
At most 9n + 2 ceil(log₂ n) + 10 logical qubits

Assumptions and source notes

Resource estimate for a specific quantum circuit over an n-bit prime field. Counts logical gates and qubits; excludes physical error correction. This is a proved circuit bound rather than a measured quantum attack.
