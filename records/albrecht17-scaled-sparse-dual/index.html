LWE / Sparse secret · dual reductions

On dual lattice attacks against small-secret LWE and parameter choices in HElib and SEAL

Martin R. Albrecht

Eurocrypt 2017

PublishedSource checked
Time bound
Scaled-dual log₂ cost ≈ [2L/(2L+1)] · (unscaled-dual log₂ cost), in the stated sparse-FHE regime
Space bound
Depends on the chosen dual-lattice solver.
Source / version date
18 May 2017
Comparison variant
Sparse secret · dual reductions
Cost scale
Parameter-dependent time and space expressions
Parameters
q(n) with log₂q=L log₂n+O(L), fixed L>0, constant Gaussian width αq=8 and fixed sparse weight h (source example h=64); source scaled dimension m′=2n.
Error distribution
Discrete Gaussian width αq=8, i.e. standard deviation 8/√(2π), independent of n.
Secret distribution
Uniform sparse ternary secret of fixed Hamming weight h, as in the source’s HElib asymptotic model.
Sample budget
Enough samples for the selected dual-lattice dimension; actual m must be enforced when instantiating.
Objective
Decision-LWE under a scaled dual-lattice attack.
Cost model
Relative exponent scaling 2L/(2L+1) against the corresponding unscaled dual model.
Assumptions
Source lattice-reduction and small-secret scaling model; fixed h and L are essential.
Success guarantee
Use the same target distinguishing advantage in both compared dual cost models.

Assumptions and source notes

A relative improvement in the source’s fixed-width sparse-FHE regime q=n^L·2^{O(L)}. It is not a universal exponent for LWE and need not beat exhaustive sparse-secret search when h is constant.

Source location

Abstract and Section Modulus switching / asymptotic behaviour

Selected earlier references

Selected sources cited by this paper.

  1. Better Key Sizes (and Attacks) for LWE-Based Encryption

    Richard Lindner · Chris Peikert · 2011

    Explains why extrapolating the empirical BKZ seconds model gives misleading asymptotic LWE estimates. Preliminaries, LP model subsection; reference RSA:LinPei11 in author source

  2. On the concrete hardness of Learning with Errors

    Martin R. Albrecht · Rachel Player · Sam Scott · 2015

    Refines earlier dual attack costing by amortizing generation of short dual relations. Preliminaries and Amortising costs; reference JMC:AlbPlaSco15

  3. Lattice Decoding Attacks on Binary LWE

    Shi Bai · Steven D. Galbraith · 2014

    Applies the earlier secret/error lattice scaling idea to a dual attack. Introduction and Modulus switching section; reference ACISP:BaiGal14
