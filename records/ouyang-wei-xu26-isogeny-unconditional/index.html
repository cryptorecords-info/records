Assumptions and source notes

The source studies the minimum degree of an isogeny to the Frobenius conjugate for a uniformly random supersingular geometric isomorphism class. Its abstract claims smoothness bounds making Wesolowski’s p^{1/3+o(1)} endomorphism-ring attack unconditional. The known reductions and random self-reducibility connect this with general supersingular isogeny search. This is a supporting-analysis record, not a further time-exponent improvement over Delgado 2026. Preprint/source-attributed claim; full proof and reduction details were not independently audited. ePrint 2026/2677 was posted later, on 29 October; chronology follows the first arXiv submission.
