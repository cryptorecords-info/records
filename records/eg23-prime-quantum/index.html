Assumptions and source notes

Extension of Regev’s method to prime-field discrete logarithms. Circuit size per run, repetitions, arithmetic implementation and post-processing are separate resources. The original construction uses number-theoretic heuristic assumptions; later unconditional variants are analysed by Pilatte.
