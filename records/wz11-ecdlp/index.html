Assumptions and source notes

A rho/negation-map method for ordinary prime-order elliptic-curve subgroups. It addresses fruitless cycles and approaches a sqrt(2) speedup, which changes the multiplicative constant but leaves the leading exponent 1/2. The random-walk model remains heuristic.
