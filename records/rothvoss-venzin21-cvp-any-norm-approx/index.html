Assumptions and source notes

Theorem 3.3 extends constant-factor approximate CVP to any norm and rank-n lattice. The approximation constant depends on ε. Lemma 3.1 removes the full-rank restriction with a controlled approximation loss.

Source location

Theorem 3.3; Lemma 3.1; §2 representation conventions
