Assumptions and source notes

Theorem 4.5 gives exact CVP in O(n^n s) arithmetic operations, where s is the input bit length. This establishes leading coefficient 1 on the n log n scale. The later n^{n/2+o(n)} CVP analysis is not backdated to Kannan.
