Assumptions and source notes

For fixed alpha, suppressing polynomial factors gives this expression from Corollary 3.3. The first arXiv abstract already states N≈Õ(n exp(alpha²n/(1−2alpha)²)) advice vectors and Õ(nN) query time. At alpha=1/4 this specializes to exp(n/4+o(n)) time and advice, or base-2 coefficient log₂(e)/4≈0.3606737602. This example is part of the same tradeoff rather than a separate result or category. All costs here are per query or stored advice after unbounded target-independent preprocessing.

Source location

Selected earlier references

Selected sources cited by this paper.

  1. Korkin-Zolotarev bases and successive minima of a lattice and its reciprocal lattice

    Jeffrey C. Lagarias · Hendrik W. Lenstra, Jr. · Claus-Peter Schnorr · 1990

    Uses the O(n^{3/2}) search approximation as its historical benchmark. §1, previous best search-CVPP approximation [LLS90]

  2. Improved Inapproximability of Lattice and Coding Problems with Preprocessing

    Oded Regev · 2004

    Separates the earlier decision approximation from search CVPP. §1, decision-CVPP history [Reg04]

  3. Lattice Problems in NP ∩ coNP

    Dorit Aharonov · Oded Regev · 2005

    Uses the decision-estimator lineage for the gradient-based search algorithm. §1 and periodic Gaussian discussion [AR05]

  4. Minkowski’s convex body theorem and integer programming

    Ravi Kannan · 1987

    Credits projection to a lattice with a close target as a reduction idea. Abstract; §1.1 and §5, reference [Kan87]

  5. On Bounded Distance Decoding for General Lattices

    Yi-Kai Liu · Vadim Lyubashevsky · Daniele Micciancio · 2006

    Improves the preprocessed hill-climbing decoder, its radius and the size of the dual-Gaussian advice. Abstract and §1.1; §3

  6. Lattice Problems in NP ∩ coNP

    Dorit Aharonov · Oded Regev · 2005

    Explains the predecessor periodic-Gaussian distance estimator and its dual-sampling representation. §1 introduction, Eq. (2)–(3)
