Assumptions and source notes

Lecture 8 fixes N = 2^{8n} log R₀ and ends by testing all pairs. The displayed 16/8 coefficients normalize this particular fixed-parameter presentation, rather than an optimized AKS bound. Nguyen–Vidick 2008, immediately after Theorem 3.1, explicitly confirms the 16 time coefficient and roughly 2^{8n} points. The course header attributes the lecture to Oded Regev and the scribing to Michael Khanevsky. Only the year is known.
