DGS / Classical · supplied basis · parallel online

An Efficient and Parallel Gaussian Sampler for Lattices

Chris Peikert

Crypto 2010

PublishedSource checked
Time bound
O(n2) online real operations; O(n2/P) per processor
Space bound
Offline matrices and fresh perturbation vector
Source / version date
2010
Comparison variant
Classical · supplied basis · parallel online
Cost scale
Online arithmetic operations
Width / covariance
Covariance Sigma > r^2 B B^T, r=omega(sqrt(log n)); spherical sufficient condition s > r s_1(B)
Offline preparation
Matrix factorization/inversion and one fresh continuous-Gaussian perturbation before each query
Output
One sample on L+c, arbitrary real shift c
Distribution error
Negligible statistical distance
Online model
Theorem 4.2: P <= n^2 processors; O(n^2/P) sufficiently precise real operations per processor

Assumptions and source notes

The scalar width is controlled by the largest singular value, not the longest Gram–Schmidt vector. Publication year is known; the detailed theorem was checked in the 2011 full author version.

Source location

Algorithm 1 and Theorem 4.2, printed pp. 11–12
Version: CRYPTO 2010 paper; checked full author version dated 13 April 2011
