Assumptions and source notes

STOC 2010 / SIAM J. Comput. 2013. The original 2011 chapter explicitly presents the 2^{2n} time / 2^n space Voronoi result. The separate 22-page author full version is dated 8 December 2010, but its first public date is unverified. A conservative public-by-2011 date is therefore supported without backdating the result to the original 2010 conference abstract.
