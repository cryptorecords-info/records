Assumptions and source notes

Decision-LWE specialization of the original free-depth corollary: choose a≈2 log₂n and b=n/a. Then q^b=2^{n+o(n)} and m=exp(O(n/log₂⁴n))=2^{o(n)}. The simplified Search-LWE Theorem 1 fixes a≈log₂n and instead gives coefficient 2; do not conflate the two statements.

Source location

Corollary 1, pp. 2–3; Corollary 3, Equations (15)–(18); distinguish simplified Search-LWE Theorem 1
