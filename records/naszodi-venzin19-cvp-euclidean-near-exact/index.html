Covering Convex Bodies and the Closest Vector Problem

Márton Naszódi · Moritz Venzin

Discrete & Computational Geometry 2022

PublishedSource checked
Time bound
2O(n) (1/δ)n/2 poly(input size, log(1/δ)), 0<δ<1
Space bound
2n poly(input size), using the general-norm constant-approximation solver
Source / version date
22 Aug 2019
Comparison variant
Approximate search · Euclidean
Cost scale
Parameterized time bound in n and approximation factor
Norm
Euclidean ℓ₂ (p=2 specialization)
Approximation guarantee
Distance at most (1+δ) dist(t,L), for 0<δ<1
Preprocessing
Included, using a general-norm 2-CVP subroutine; covering generated locally
Success guarantee
Randomized success at least 1-2^{-n}; deterministic net enumeration is also discussed

Assumptions and source notes

The Euclidean specialization p=2 of the smoothness-based covering algorithm improves the dependence on approximation accuracy. This is an alternative algorithmic bound rather than a claim to beat the already known 2^{n+o(n)} exact Euclidean CVP bound. No numerical leading coefficient is inferred from the hidden 2^{O(n)} factor.

Source location

Abstract; §1, item (5); Theorem 4.6, using the ℓ₂ modulus of smoothness with power q=2
