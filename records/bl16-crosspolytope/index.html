Assumptions and source notes

AFRICACRYPT 2016; first ePrint 24 August 2015. Independently reaches the 0.298 time level through efficiently computable cross-polytope hashes, improving practicality over SphereSieve. The abstract applies to arbitrary and ideal lattices. A numeric space coefficient is not inferred from the abstract; consult the chosen variant. Theorem 1 gives 0.2972, often rounded to 0.298 in the abstract; this is a precision normalization, not a new algorithmic improvement.

Source location

Theorem 1; identical asymptotic collision analysis to spherical LSH
