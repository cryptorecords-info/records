Assumptions and source notes

Reduces dimension n to a few sieve calls in dimensions below n − d, where d = Θ(n/log n). Applying this to the cited spherical-filter sieve preserves its leading coefficient ½log₂(3/2), while improving the subexponential factor. The row therefore ties the standing exponent; it is not a new exponent record. The paper also implements a simpler (4/3)^n sieve. No separate total-space coefficient is inferred from the checked abstract.
