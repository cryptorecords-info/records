Comparison rules
Use q=Θ(n²), Gaussian width s=Θ(n), and exactly the displayed asymptotic sample budget. Constant Gaussian normalizations do not change these exponents. These are interior parameters c_q=2,c_s=1; they are not the Regev q≈n²,width≈n^{3/2}/polylog(n) regime.
